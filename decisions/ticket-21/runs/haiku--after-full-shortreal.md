WHAT WORKS
The urgency is grounded. "Project grants are 80% of Client E's income (2024 accounts) and each is single-year. Two of five grants are not expected to renew, per the funder letters on file." This moves the board from nice-to-have to need-to-do.

The go/no-go discipline is excellent. A soft launch to 200 people with a 5% threshold before full launch shows the team understands how to test without over-committing.

The base is real. 1,400 mailing contacts at 32% event attendance is genuine capacity to build on, and comparable pricing at 40-60 pounds validates the 45 pound point.

WHAT TO FIX, IN ORDER

1. The coordinator does not know how to actually run a membership on the information given. [High impact]
   Where: Slides 7 and 10
   Quote: "Priority booking, two members-only events a year and a discount with local partners" and "Month one: soft launch to 200 contacts."
   Why it costs the client: If the board approves this and the coordinator says "I do not know how to start," the recommendation stalls. The board is making a decision based on a plan the person executing it cannot execute. That is the recommendation being too vague to act on.
   What to do: Add to Slide 7 one more bullet: the actual operational steps. How are members signed up (form, email, existing database)? Which events become members-only and when? What partner discount is realistic to negotiate and by when? Slide 10 can stay focused on the timeline, but Slide 7 needs to say what the coordinator actually builds in month one.
   Answer first: Can the coordinator set up membership enrollment and communicate the benefit to the 200 in month one with the existing tooling, or does the team need to scope that out more?

2. The revenue comparator is missing. [Minor]
   Where: Slide 8
   Quote: "If 8% of the mailing list joins at 45 pounds, that is about 5,000 pounds in year one."
   Why it costs the client: The board does not know whether 5,000 pounds matters. Is it 5% of lost grant income or 50%? The context (two grants at risk) was on an earlier slide, but the deck never connects the two. This does not change the decision to launch, but it changes what success looks like in year one.
   What to do: State what the 5,000 replaces. "The two at-risk grants totaled £X in 2024. Year one membership revenue of £5,000 replaces Y% of that gap." Then the board knows what to expect in year two and whether the member growth curve needs to accelerate.
   Answer first: What was the annual value of the two at-risk grants, and what does growth to 12% conversion look like in year two?

THE HABIT TO BUILD
The team is testing assumptions in a soft launch, which is smart. The habit to build next is working backwards from "Who executes this?" to "Does this person have what they need?" Your title and timeline are clear. The operational plan is not. The discipline is: before you hand a recommendation to the client, hand it to the person who will execute it first and ask "Is this enough?" If they have a question, it is a finding in your deck.

WHAT THIS REVIEW COULD NOT JUDGE
Whether Client E already has a membership management system or the infrastructure to set one up in month one.
Whether the local partner organizations have capacity to offer meaningful discounts.
Whether two members-only events a year is feasible on top of the regular program.
What the team learned from researching the three comparable organizations and whether they use software that Client E could adopt.
Whether the mailing list is as accurate and engaged as assumed.

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING THE TEAM
What specific software or process will the coordinator use to enroll, track and manage members?
Which two events in the calendar will be designated members-only, and have you checked with whoever runs the events program that this is feasible?
If the soft launch converts below 5%, what is the pivot? (Do you try a different price, a different audience, a different messaging?)
Who are the three comparable groups and do they use systems or a process the team already knows works?
In month two, how will you measure the 8% target against the soft launch results?
