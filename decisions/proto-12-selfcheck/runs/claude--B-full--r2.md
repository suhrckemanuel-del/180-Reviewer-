THE HABIT TO BUILD
The weak habit: presenting a number as the answer without showing what it should be measured against. The reusable lesson: before you show a figure to a decision-maker, ask three questions: Compared to what? So is that good or bad? And if it is bad, what is it a symptom of? If you cannot answer all three, the figure is not ready. Exercise: Take three financial figures from a real deck (revenue, growth rate, conversion rate) and for each one, name what it should be compared against (prior period, market peer, client target, industry benchmark). If you cannot name it, you have found the missing piece your deck needs.
