# Grading a finding

Each finding gets one of three labels. **Start at Minor** and move up only when the evidence
earns it. Severity belongs to the finding, never to the deck.

## Minor (the default)

The deck is already sound on this point. The finding strengthens it: a line to add, a benchmark to
caveat, a figure to put in context. The client could act without it. Label its next step
"optional".

## Moderate

A real gap the team closes before the deck goes out, because closing it changes the
recommendation or how well it holds up. Promote to Moderate only when quoted evidence shows one of:

- **two actions:** the client would do two different things depending on this issue. Name both.
- **defensibility:** the evidence materially weakens the existing recommendation.

"Could affect the decision" is not enough. If the stage or the dependency is unknown, write it as
an **Ask the team** question, and do not assume an imminent rollout or a binding price.

## High impact

The client could be misled, or the recommendation is wrong or unsupported. High impact needs one
of these five, shown with quotes:

1. **A contradiction the recommendation rests on.** Only when the client cannot resolve it from the
   deck and the two readings lead to different actions. Write both: "under one reading the client
   does X, under the other Y". If you cannot, or one action is "ask the team", grade it on its
   own merits. Two slides citing different figures for one quantity is usually Minor.
2. **No clear decision.** The deck does not answer the client's question, or the core
   recommendation is unsupported.
3. **The wrong problem.** It answers a different question, or the framing drifts.
4. **An ungrounded headline number** that drives the decision.
5. **A confidentiality or safety breach** inside the deliverable (a real person's private data).

## Decision stage

A small pilot testing a labelled assumption is not a committed rollout relying on it as fact. A
labelled assumption is not an unsupported claim. On strong work, keep the review short and lead
with what works. The 5-finding limit is a ceiling, never a target.
