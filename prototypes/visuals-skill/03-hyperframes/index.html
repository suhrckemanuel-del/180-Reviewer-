Missed pickups · method

How the numbers on these slides were computed

Missed pickup: a booked parcel nobody collected by closing time.

Rate: missed pickups divided by booked pickups, per month and channel.

Jul–Dec totals: 306 missed, 173 of them phone bookings.

Every number on these slides comes from recompute.py run on the booking log.
