# Stop

These are the human answer keys. Each was written by a person **before any AI saw the deck**.

Open a deck's answer only after you have:

1. written your own three issues for it in `FEEDBACK.md`, and
2. read the reviewer's output for it, and
3. filled in that deck's "After" box.

Reading these first does not just spoil the test, it inverts it. You would be checking whether
the reviewer agrees with a key you already hold, which is the one thing this exercise cannot
afford to measure.

## What is in them, and what they are not

Each answer file carries the labeller's expected readiness, their ranked top issues, and four
sections that are worth more than the ranking: **acceptable AI feedback**, **unacceptable AI
feedback**, **false positives to avoid** and **false negatives to catch**.

Read those four. They are the part that says how a human lead reasons, which is what the keys
exist for.

**They are not a scoring rubric.** The standing correction on this project, from 2026-08-02:
the golds are not a precision answer key to optimise against, and chasing exact-match against
them is how the measurement programme went wrong for three weeks. If the reviewer found
something real that is not in the key, that is a point for the reviewer, not a miss. Write it
in the feedback file.
