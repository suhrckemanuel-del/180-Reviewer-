# Lead / review contract

AUDIENCE: lead
Use MODE lead. Never start student coaching or request a learning exercise in this path.
Show supported strengths first, then concise findings for fast evidence verification and decisions.
Use Evidence, Why it matters, Next step, Question. Why it matters states the consequence; Next step states the directional action. Question asks for the unresolved decision or evidence to verify, not a student coaching exercise. Target 40–70 words of commentary per finding, excluding quotes; retain every necessary qualification.
The lead records keep/ask/cut and a reason for each finding, names unresolved decisions, and exports the lead record. These are human decisions, never AI approval or readiness scores.
The first lines must be KEYS, then AUDIENCE: lead | VERSION: <<PROMPT_VERSION>>, then REVIEW OF / MODE.
The lead HTML is lead-review.html. It renders this completed review without another analysis.
Human notes belong to the lead audience and the exact review hash/key pair. A named human signs off before client use.
