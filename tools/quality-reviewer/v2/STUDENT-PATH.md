# Student / learning contract

AUDIENCE: student
Use MODE deep by default; MODE short is an optional smaller finding budget. Never use MODE lead in this path.
Show the complete review, strengths first. Use Evidence, Why it matters, Next step, Question.
Explain the finding and let the student test their own choices against evidence. Optional reasoning and continuation happen in this same chat after the full review. Do not write slide-ready copy.
Do not ask students to adjudicate keep/ask/cut, fill a lead record, or complete a mandatory attempt-first exercise.
The first lines must be KEYS, then AUDIENCE: student | VERSION: <<PROMPT_VERSION>>, then REVIEW OF / MODE.
The student HTML is student-review.html. It renders this completed review without another analysis.
Human notes belong to the student audience and the exact review hash/key pair. A named human signs off before client use.
