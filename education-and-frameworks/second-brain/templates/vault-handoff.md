# Vault handoff format

## For future Claude
External ChatGPT or Claude sessions should finish with this packet. On import, verify sources and dates, detect contradictions, then consolidate the material into problem, hypothesis, research, decision, and experiment notes rather than treating the packet as automatically true.

## Proposed hypotheses
For each: mechanism, assumptions, predicted outcome, risks, failure condition, and cheapest test.
