# D-001 - Prove adoption before seeking a formal provider partnership

## For future Claude
The recommended path is to begin with an affordable or discounted controlled pilot, then approach global 180DC and a provider with evidence. Do not present one branch as an established Anthropic, OpenAI, or Cursor implementation partner without authorization and delivery proof.

## Proposed decision

Pursue the partnership in stages:

1. **Clarify authority and eligibility**
   - Determine the legal entity behind the branch.
   - Ask who may purchase organizational plans and sign agreements.
   - Check whether global 180DC or the university must sponsor the relationship.

2. **Run a controlled internal pilot**
   - Use no client-confidential material.
   - Select a defined consultant or builder cohort.
   - Choose two or three bounded workflows.
   - Provide training, rules, and examples.
   - Assign an administrator and escalation owner.

3. **Measure the pilot**
   - Adoption and repeat usage
   - User capability before and after training
   - Quality or time improvements in agreed tasks
   - Unsupported-output and privacy incidents
   - User and project-lead feedback
   - Workflows where the tool should not be used

4. **Create a partnership brief**
   - 180DC mission and reach
   - Existing global technology-partnership precedent
   - Pilot evidence
   - Proposed cohort and use cases
   - Governance and training model
   - Mutual provider value
   - Exact request

5. **Seek global 180DC sponsorship**
   - Frame the opportunity as an in-kind technology and professional-development partnership.
   - Use the existing global partnerships process.
   - Avoid bypassing global brand and agreement ownership.

6. **Approach the best-fit provider**
   - Anthropic: nonprofit access, Education team, Campus program, then broader partnership.
   - OpenAI: nonprofit Business access, university Edu route, then sales.
   - Cursor: student access for builders; organization discussion only after a clear technical program exists.

## Recommended first provider to test

Anthropic currently has the most visibly aligned combination of nonprofit pricing, education programming, student Builder Clubs, AI-fluency resources, and a formal partner network. This makes it the strongest first conversation if 180DC eligibility and internal approval are confirmed.

OpenAI is a close alternative and may be stronger if shared custom GPTs, general consultant access, data analysis, and Codex are central to the pilot.

Cursor should be treated as a specialist builder tool, not the organization-wide consulting platform.

## Minimum credible outreach request

Ask for a limited pilot relationship, not a sweeping strategic partnership:

- Discounted or sponsored seats for a defined cohort
- One provider-led workshop or speaker
- AI-fluency resources
- Limited builder credits where relevant
- A feedback session after the pilot
- Option to discuss a broader global partnership if agreed outcomes are met

## Reversal conditions

Do not proceed if:

- Authority or legal eligibility is unclear.
- Client-confidentiality controls are not ready.
- There is no defined workflow or pilot owner.
- The board or global organization does not support the outreach.
- The proposal primarily seeks free access without measurable mutual value.
