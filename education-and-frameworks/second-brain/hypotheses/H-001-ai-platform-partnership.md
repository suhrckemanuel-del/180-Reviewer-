# H-001 - AI platform partnership for 180DC

## For future Claude
Manuel is exploring whether Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, or another AI provider could supply organizational access, training, credits, or support to 180DC. The strongest version is an in-kind technology and learning partnership, not an attempt to outsource AI governance or force one tool into every workflow.

## Hypothesis

A provider may support a controlled 180DC AI adoption program because 180DC offers:

- A global student and emerging-talent network
- Access to socially focused consulting use cases
- A natural education and AI-fluency mission
- Structured workshops and feedback
- Potential case studies about responsible AI use
- A pathway from internal consultant enablement to nonprofit-client impact

## Possible partnership package

- Organizational seats or credits
- AI-fluency training
- Guest speakers or workshops
- Builder support for a small technical team
- Office hours or implementation guidance
- Responsible-use templates
- Product feedback from students and project teams
- An anonymized impact case after a successful pilot

## What 180DC would contribute

- A named pilot cohort and accountable owner
- Training attendance and adoption support
- Strict rules against uploading client-confidential information
- Defined use cases and measurable outcomes
- Structured feedback
- Appropriate brand visibility if approved
- A case study only with provider, 180DC, and client permissions

## Main risks

- Partnership branding arrives before useful adoption.
- Consultants upload confidential material without adequate controls.
- Tool access substitutes for learning or weakens consulting judgment.
- The branch becomes locked into one provider prematurely.
- The provider expects scale or commercial delivery the branch cannot support.
- Local outreach bypasses global 180DC partnership ownership.

## Validation required

- Confirm legal and organizational eligibility.
- Secure branch and, where required, global approval.
- Select a small non-confidential pilot.
- Demonstrate useful adoption and training completion.
- Measure outcomes and incidents.
- Define the mutual value proposition before provider outreach.
