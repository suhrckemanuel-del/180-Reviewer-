# Independent review and stress test

Purpose: challenge the consulting applicability and the proposed three-context workshop after the firm research is complete. This is a qualitative adversarial review, not a controlled trial with consultants or a historical financial backtest.

Each reviewer should read Consulting_AI_Research_Findings.md, inspect relevant primary sources, and assess the eight candidate statements below without assuming they are correct. Return for each: supported, qualified, unsupported, or contradicted; correction; why it matters. Retain legitimate statements. Then give a verdict on the design, the three most material weaknesses, concrete changes, and what remains untested.

## Candidate statements

1. McKinsey reports colleague time savings of up to 30% in searching and synthesizing knowledge; this is not a promised time saving for our workshop.
2. The BCG experiment proves current AI research tools make consultants 25.1% faster across all consulting work.
3. Bain's 2024 deployment account documents ChatGPT Enterprise tools for Python data analysis and automating Excel processes.
4. Three accounts agreeing on a claim constitutes independent corroboration, even when all cite the same original announcement.
5. Kantar's account describes analysts adding contextual knowledge from first-hand respondent interaction to AI interpretation of transcripts.
6. Forrester's client-facing AI access product proves that all its underlying research is autonomously authored by AI.
7. Public evidence that convenience retail is growing is enough to recommend Northstar invest its entire £10m in new shops without inspecting its store economics.
8. Keeping the final recommendation human-written guarantees junior consultants keep developing research judgment and guarantees no workforce replacement.

## Reviewer outputs

The two reviews were completed independently in fresh contexts after the firm research draft. The first-principles reviewer checked five original sources; the overall reviewer checked six. Both recommended a bounded pilot with revisions, which have been incorporated into the findings.

| Statement | First-principles reviewer | Overall reviewer | Root assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: McKinsey attributed, scoped savings | Supported | Supported | Retain attribution, task scope, and upper-bound wording |
| 2: universal current 25.1% speedup | Contradicted | Contradicted | Restrict to the studied GPT-4 tasks; other tasks showed worse correctness |
| 3: Bain Python and Excel examples | Supported | Supported | Retain as documented firm practice |
| 4: account agreement is corroboration | Contradicted | Contradicted | Count evidence origins and inspect originals |
| 5: Kantar adds respondent context | Supported | Supported | Retain as a concrete human contribution |
| 6: Forrester AI proves autonomous authorship | Contradicted | Contradicted | Distinguish access to research from creation by experts |
| 7: sector growth justifies all capital | Unsupported | Unsupported | Obtain client economics and compare alternatives |
| 8: human writing guarantees skills and jobs | Unsupported | Unsupported | Require observable practice; no employment guarantee established |

The root reviewed both reports and original sources. The revised design audits coverage as well as claims, connects evidence to client data needs, limits the combined ledger, assigns human owners, measures review and handoff costs, and preserves unaided reasoning and later transfer checks.

The test retained three legitimate statements and rejected five overclaims. This is a diagnostic result on deliberately selected examples, not a benchmark accuracy, error reduction, observed consultant time saving, retained learning result, or workforce outcome. The agents share a model family and can share blind spots. A human pilot comparing manual, single-chat, and three-context workflows remains necessary before asserting relative performance.
