## Best 10 for a student consultancy (180DC)

1. **Claude plugins / Skills for the 180DC methodology (#4).** Encodes the team's standard once and every consultant inherits it. It is the highest-leverage use of a paid seat.
2. **NotebookLM (#20).** A free or cheap source-grounded synthesis tool with citations. It is the safest research tool for juniors.
3. **Claude for Excel (#1).** It keeps models native and auditable, which matters more for the client than a pretty chart.
4. **Claude for PowerPoint (#2), or Gemini in Slides (#22) on Google Workspace.** It writes into the team's real template, so there is no reformatting.
5. **A deep-research tool, one only (#5, #17, #21 or #24).** Pick one: Perplexity Pro has the most runs per dollar, and Claude Research works if the team already pays for Claude.
6. **Claude Cowork (#3).** It hands off whole errands (folder to model plus deck), and one Pro seat covers it.
7. **Interview synthesis with quote traceability (#29).** Use Claude Projects or NotebookLM first, and only pay for Dovetail if volume justifies it.
8. **Claude Artifacts (#7).** A clickable dashboard or calculator in minutes makes a recommendation tangible.
9. **ChatGPT data analysis (#18).** The fastest way for non-coders to clean and explore client data, provided they show the code.
10. **Claude Code + Routines for one technical member (#8, #11, #12).** Use it for prototypes, batch deliverables and the automated status digest. This is one power user, not the whole team.

## Not worth it (or hype) for 180DC

- **Building custom agents on the Agent SDK (#13)** without a technical owner. It is a maintenance liability, and the student role turns over every semester.
- **Gamma and Beautiful.ai for client decks (#26, #27).** They look slick, but they produce generic, low-fidelity PPTX. Fine for internal or recruiting use, wrong for a consulting deliverable.
- **Paying for several deep-research subscriptions at once.** The outputs overlap heavily, and the bottleneck is human source-checking, not report volume.
- **ChatGPT agent mode on Plus (#16)** as a core workflow. The roughly 40/month cap is too small, and it is slower than doing the task in Cowork or by hand.
- **HyperFrames videos (#31)** as a default deliverable. It is a nice-to-have for a showcase readout, but a poor return on hours for most projects.
- **Enterprise survey AI (Qualtrics Text iQ, #30)** for small-n NGO surveys. A Claude or ChatGPT pass over the CSV export does the same job.
- **Microsoft Copilot (#23)** unless the university already provides the licence. Buying it only for this duplicates Claude for Excel and PowerPoint.
- **Perplexity Comet (#25).** Browser-switching for marginal gain, and the injection risk is the same as other browser agents.
